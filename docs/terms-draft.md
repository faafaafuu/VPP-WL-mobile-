# Terms Of Service Draft

Status: draft for legal review. Do not publish as final legal text without counsel approval for the target jurisdictions and app stores.

## Service

VPN Router is a subscription VPN routing service for mobile devices. Availability, speed, regions, and supported protocols may change as nodes are added, removed, blocked, degraded, or maintained.

## Acceptable Use

Users must not use the service for:

- illegal activity;
- abuse, spam, credential attacks, malware distribution, or network attacks;
- violating third-party rights;
- bypassing payment, rate limits, or technical controls;
- reselling access without written permission.

The service may suspend accounts or block access to protect the platform, users, infrastructure providers, and legal obligations.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple App Store or Google Play unless another approved channel is explicitly offered. Store subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through the relevant store account settings.

Prices, trial periods, renewal dates, refunds, and cancellation handling follow the rules of the store where the subscription was purchased.

The app must show privacy information and subscription terms before purchase.

## Availability

The service is provided on a best-effort basis. VPN nodes may be unavailable due to maintenance, provider outages, network filtering, protocol blocking, or force majeure events.

The client may automatically switch nodes or protocols when available, but uninterrupted connectivity is not guaranteed.

## No Misuse Of Privacy Claims

The service does not store traffic content logs, but users remain responsible for their use of the service. Privacy features do not authorize illegal conduct.

## Changes

Features, routing rules, nodes, protocols, prices, and these terms may change. Material changes should be communicated through the app, website, or store listing where required.

## Termination

Users may stop using the service and cancel subscriptions through the applicable store. The service may terminate or restrict access for abuse, non-payment, legal requirements, or security risk.

The final production terms must define liability limits, warranty disclaimers, governing law, dispute process, and mandatory consumer rights language for target jurisdictions.
